Nakata for iOS

A private AI chat app: bring your own API key from OpenRouter, OpenAI, Groq, DeepSeek or Google Gemini, and chat with it directly from your iPhone. Your key and your chats stay on your device.

Getting started

  1. Open Nakata. Settings opens on first launch.
  2. Choose a provider and paste your API key (each provider links to the page where you create one).
  3. Tap Save and send a message.

Common questions

"Bağlantı: HTTP 401/403" — the key is wrong or revoked; create a new one at your provider.
"HTTP 402" or "Insufficient balance" — your provider account needs credit.
"HTTP 429/503" — the provider is rate-limiting or busy; wait a moment or pick another model in Settings.
