Nakata for iOS

An AI chat app for iPhone. Chat for free on the built-in model, or connect your own API key from OpenRouter, OpenAI, Groq, DeepSeek or Google Gemini. Your chats stay on your device; see the Privacy Policy for what goes where.

Getting started

  1. Open Nakata and send a message: the free model needs no key (up to 30 messages a day).
  2. To use your own provider, open Settings, choose it and paste your API key (each provider links to the page where you create one).
  3. To create images with credits, sign in with your nakata.app account under Account.

Common questions

"Bağlantı: HTTP 401/403" — the key is wrong or revoked; create a new one at your provider.
"HTTP 402" or "Insufficient balance" — your provider account needs credit.
"HTTP 429/503" — the provider is rate-limiting or busy; wait a moment or pick another model in Settings.
